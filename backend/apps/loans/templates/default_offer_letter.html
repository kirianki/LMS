6. INTEREST & PENALTIES

(a) Interest Rate: The facility shall attract interest at the approved rate indicated in Section 2. The Lender reserves the right to review the interest rate in line with market conditions upon giving 30 days' written notice.
(b) Late Payment Penalty: Any installment or principal amount not paid on its due date shall attract a default penalty of 10% per month (or such other rate as may be advised) on the overdue amount until full settlement.
(c) Accrual: Interest and penalties shall continue to accrue after as well as before any judgment.

7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

The Lender shall be entitled to demand immediate repayment of the entire outstanding balance (Principal + Interest + Penalties) if any of the following events occur:
  1. The Borrower fails to pay any installment on the due date.
  2. The Borrower provides false, misleading, or incorrect information during the application.
  3. The Borrower becomes bankrupt, insolvent, or admits inability to pay debts.
  4. The Borrower is convicted of a criminal offense involving dishonesty or fraud.
  5. Any distress or execution is levied against the Borrower's assets.

8. REPRESENTATIONS & WARRANTIES

The Borrower represents and warrants that:
(a) They have the power and authority to enter into this agreement and perform obligations hereunder.
(b) This agreement constitutes a valid, legal, and binding obligation enforceable in accordance with its terms.
(c) There are no pending or threatened legal proceedings that could materially affect their ability to repay this facility.

9. NOTICES

Any notice or demand required to be given under this agreement shall be deemed sufficiently served if sent by SMS, Email, or Physical Letter to the contact details provided by the Borrower in the application form.

10. VARIATION & INDULGENCE

(a) No variation of this agreement shall be valid unless in writing and signed by both parties.
(b) No relaxation, forbearance, or indulgence by the Lender in enforcing any of the terms hereof shall prejudice or restrict the rights of the Lender under this agreement.

11. COSTS & EXPENSES

The Borrower hereby agrees to indemnify the Lender on demand against all costs, charges, and expenses (including legal fees on a solicitor-client basis and debt collector's commissions) incurred by the Lender in enforcing or attempting to enforce its rights under this facility.

The Borrower expressly consents to the Lender:
(a) Submitting information regarding this facility and the Borrower's repayment performance to licensed Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs).
(b) Obtaining credit reports from CRBs for the purpose of credit assessment and monitoring.
